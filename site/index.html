      How it works

      Source

      Every month the Receita Federal publishes the full Brazilian company registry (CNPJ) as open data. Each week this site checks for a new release and scans every company opened in the last 12 months that is still active.

      Selection

      A company is kept when it declares both a technology activity (software, platforms, data) and a creative one (fashion, beauty, jewellery, design, film, music, arts, publishing), when its name points to the other side, or when it is registered under Inova Simples, Brazil's startup regime. Individual micro-entrepreneurs (MEI), branches and closed companies are left out.

      Websites

      When the company's registered email uses its own domain, the site visits that domain each month to see whether a website is live and links to Instagram or LinkedIn. Domains shared by many new companies (usually accountants) are ignored. LinkedIn itself is never scraped: use the LinkedIn search link on each company.

      Limits

      The score reads public signals, not quality. Many funded Brazilian startups are held by a company abroad, so the local entity may carry an unfamiliar name. Registry data is published monthly, so new companies arrive in monthly batches; websites are checked weekly.

      Data: Receita Federal do Brasil, Dados Abertos CNPJ. Companies are placed at the centre of their head office's city.
